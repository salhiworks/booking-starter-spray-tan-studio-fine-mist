# Photos — Overcast Tan Studio (Fine Mist)

One studio look, one model per frame: white robes and towel turbans against a pale dusty-blue wall,
soft daylight, skin in close-up, a fine mist from a spray bottle. Two Pexels series shot in the same
vocabulary and graded together:

- **Sora Shimazaki** — a blue studio series (Pexels 5938522–5938648): a model with medium-deep brown
  skin in a white robe and towel turban; hands on skin; a fine mist sprayed onto an open palm.
- **Karolina Grabowska (Kaboompics)** — a vanity series (Pexels 7321228–7321545): a model with fair
  skin in a white robe at a white table with a round mirror, a mist spray and cream on skin.

Nobody is shown spray tanned, sprayed by an artist or "after". No couples, nothing suggestive, no
beach. Licence: the **Pexels License** (free to use, no attribution required; not to be sold
unaltered or used to imply endorsement). Credit is given here anyway.

These are **stock photographs, not the sample business**. The site labels every frame "Sample
photo". Never describe them as Overcast's tanning artist, its studio, a client or a client's result;
never use them as a before-and-after. The owner's own photos (of people who agreed to be shown)
replace them.

Processed by `concepts/spray-tan-studio-fine-mist/photo-pipeline/photos.mjs` in the Niche Pack
workspace (crop + a grade per series that brings both walls to one dusty blue: skin kept true —
never warmed or pushed toward orange — a touch of contrast; the booking-band frame is mirrored; no
retouching). Only the processed AVIF/WebP crops are in `public/photos/` — no originals.
